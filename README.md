# AeroTrack – Flight Training Records Management System

AeroTrack is a Python-based web application designed to help flight schools manage students, instructors, aircraft, training records, and regulatory compliance. The goal is to replace paper-based record keeping with a secure, centralized, and easy-to-use digital system.

## Project Goals

* Digitize student training records
* Simplify flight scheduling
* Track student progress and flight hours
* Manage instructor assignments
* Monitor aircraft availability and maintenance
* Store certificates, licenses, and important documents
* Generate reports for students, instructors, and administrators
